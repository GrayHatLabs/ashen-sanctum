# Endgame (plan, 2026-10-07)

**Decided with the user:**
- build all four parts;
- Ash Rifts open after Normal (after Solanthos the first time).

**What exists today:** Normal → Nightmare → Hell, each a fresh run through all six acts with the same hero.
After Hell there's nothing new to chase.

**Goal:** a reason to keep playing a finished hero, in the spirit of Diablo II's boss runs and later ARPGs'
endless rifts. It should work on handhelds: short sessions, pad-friendly.

## 1. Boss runs: the Rekindling

Every town gets a **brazier**, lit once that act's last boss falls. Using it **rekindles the act**:
- every dungeon in the act is rebuilt with a new layout and fresh monsters;
- all its bosses come back.

You can farm a favourite boss (each boss has its own unique item) without replaying the story. It's free,
works on any difficulty, and the drop rates are those of the difficulty you're on.

## 2. Ancient items: a rarity above Unique

**Ancients** are a deep orange-red rarity, very rare:
- **Where they drop:** only from Hell bosses and rift guardians.
- **What they are:** a unique's stats rolled **25-50% stronger**, plus one **ancient power**: a build-changing
  effect such as:
  - fireballs split in three;
  - your thralls explode on death;
  - the whip's crack chains to a second foe;
  - holy fire spreads on kill;
  - your summons are giants.
- **About 16 ancient powers,** two per hero.
- The jeweler can't socket them, so they're kept as found.

## 3. Ash Rifts: endless challenge maps

**The Riftwarden,** a new NPC in Windward Anchorage after Hell (or after Normal, see the questions), opens
**Ash Rifts**:
- **Tiers:** numbered (Tier 1, 2, 3...), endless, and each one harder.
- **The map:** one randomly assembled dungeon floor using any act's look and monsters, mixed (sea monsters
  beside clockwork ones).
- **Modifiers:** a few per rift, more at higher tiers. For example:
  - *Burning Ground* (fire patches);
  - *Swift* (monsters +30% speed);
  - *Teeming* (+50% monsters);
  - *Vampiric* (monsters heal on hit);
  - *Fragile* (you take +25%, they drop +50%);
  - *Eclipse* (your light shrinks);
  - *Gale* (the Skyreach's wind, with edges);
  - *Tidal* (the deep's tides).
- **The guardian:** kill enough monsters to fill the **rift bar** and a **guardian** appears: a past boss,
  stronger, with a modifier of its own.
- **The prize:** gold, items, and a chance at an **ancient** that grows with the tier. Clearing a tier within
  its timer unlocks the next. Your **best tier per hero** shows on the hero card.

## 4. Embers of mastery: soft progression after level 50

After level 50, experience fills **Ember** levels instead of character levels. Each Ember is a small permanent
choice from four tracks:
- +2% damage;
- +2% life;
- +3% gold and magic find;
- +1% move speed.

Each track is capped, so it never runs away. This gives every rift run some progress even without a drop.
