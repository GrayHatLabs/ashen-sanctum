# Acts 5 and 6: The Drowned Deep and The Shattered Heavens (plan, 2026-10-06)

The user, 2026-10-06: "let's do two more acts: Act 5 Drowned Deep, Act 6 Shattered Heavens."

Both are built like Acts 2 to 4:
- an overland with a town;
- three herald dungeons, each holding a relic;
- a final dungeon that needs all three relics;
- art, music, quests, bot runs and snapshots.

**Nightmare and Hell** move to after Act 6's final boss.

## The story thread

The Clockmaker was winding the world, but not for himself.
- **The sea:** the ash of four acts has been falling into the sea for an age. Something old down there has been eating it and growing.
- **Act 5:** the Clockmaker's last gear opens a way down to that thing.
- **The sky:** killing it drains the deep, and shows where the ash has always come from.
- **Act 6:** a burnt-out god, high above the clouds, in the real **Ashen Sanctum**, the place the game is named for.

## Act 5: The Drowned Deep

**Unlock:** after the Clockmaker. His broken engine falls through the floor of the Clockwork Dominion into the sea, and a diving-bell lift waits in the Last Escapement.

**Look:**
- Abyssal teal and black, lit by glowing cyan and violet sea life.
- Pale coral, rusted shipwrecks, kelp forests and pearl-white bone.
- Drifting particles: bubbles and "sea snow".

### Town: Brinehollow

A village of stilt houses built on the hull of a giant wrecked ship, inside a huge air bubble on the sea floor.
- **Captain Ysolde Marrow:** a one-eyed salvager who tells the story.
- **Nessa the pearl-diver:** potions, food and gear.
- **Brother Coral:** a tide-priest who heals and resets skills.
- **A jeweler,** as in every town, plus divers and fishwives.

### Overland: The Sunken Reach

- **Terrain:**
  - the sea floor under a glowing bubble-dome;
  - kelp forests, coral reefs and shipwrecks;
  - trenches and whale bones;
  - a road back up to the lift.
- **Tides, the act's mechanic:**
  - every 60 to 90 seconds a warning bell sounds and the tide comes in for about 20 seconds;
  - low ground floods: you and land monsters wade at -30% speed, while sea monsters swim faster and get bolder;
  - high ground (rocks, coral, wreck decks) stays dry;
  - shown on the map, with a tide gauge in the HUD.

### Monsters

- **Drowned Sailors:** waterlogged shamblers. They drag you (a brief slow) and come back once unless burned.
- **Merrow Spearmen:** fish-folk who lunge out of the flood. They're stronger during high tide.
- **Anglerlurks:** they hide, showing only a glowing lure that looks like a pickup, then ambush.
- **Jelly Drifts:** floating swarms that shock whatever touches them.
- **Shellguard Crabs:** they block hits from the front, so flank them or use area damage.
- **Sirens:** their song charms you and pulls you toward them. Kill or interrupt them.
- **Ink Horrors:** they spray ink clouds that blind (darken your vision).

### Dungeons and heralds (the relics are three **Leviathan Pearls**)

1. **The Wreck of the Sovereign** (2 floors): **Admiral Dregmoor**, a drowned admiral.
   - Cannon volleys and a thrown anchor.
   - Calls up his drowned crew.
2. **The Coral Cathedral** (2 floors): **Mother Nacre**, a pearl-crowned siren queen.
   - A charm song that pulls you in.
   - Coral spikes burst from the floor; mirror-pearls reflect bolts.
3. **The Midnight Trench** (3 floors, very dark): **the Angler Matriarch**.
   - She puts out the lights.
   - Only her lure glows, and she lunges from the dark.
4. **The Drowned Sanctum** (3 floors, needs the three Pearls): **the Leviathan**.
   - **Phase 1:** its coils sweep across the arena (body segments you must jump between).
   - **Phase 2:** a tidal wave floods the arena. Stand on the raised altars; whirlpools pull you in.
   - **Phase 3:** its head rises. Strike its eyes while it breathes a pressure beam.

## Act 6: The Shattered Heavens

**Unlock:** after the Leviathan. The drained sea shows a stair of light rising from the Sanctum's floor, and the Sky Pirate's kind of airship is moored at the top.

**Look:**
- Floating islands above an endless ash-cloud sea.
- Pale gold, cracked white marble, storm-violet and sunset orange.
- Broken halos and colossal angel statues; ash falling *upward*.

### Town: Windward Anchorage

A sky-harbour of islands chained together, with airships docked.
- **Seraphine:** a deserter angel with clipped wings who tells the story.
- **Quartermaster Bram:** an old sky-pirate merchant.
- **Sister Aurel:** a healer who resets skills.
- **A jeweler,** plus deckhands.

### Overland: The Skyreach

- **Islands:** linked by **chain bridges**, plus **airship docks** that hop you between distant islands (short rides that work like portals).
- **Wind, the act's mechanic:**
  - gusts sweep across the islands, shown by streaks;
  - a gust pushes you and monsters;
  - your projectiles fly further and faster with the wind, and shorter against it.
- **Edges:** the islands have real edges.
  - Being knocked or blown off costs about 15% of your life.
  - You land back at the last bridge or dock.
  - Monsters knocked off are simply gone, with no XP. So knockbacks become a tactic: the Valkyrie, the Berserker and the Inquisitor's Hook all shine here.

### Monsters

- **Fallen Seraphs:** broken-winged angels with spears of light. They dive at you from above.
- **Ophanim:** wheels of eyes that turn slowly and sweep beams.
- **Storm Drakes:** small lightning-breathing drakes.
- **Ash Harpies:** fast flocks that knock you back (watch the edges).
- **Gilded Sentinels:** colossal statues that wake when you pass. Slow, very tough, ground slams.
- **Sun-Zealots:** cultists who heal and shield the others.
- **Thunderbirds:** rare elites that call down lightning strikes.

### Dungeons and heralds (the relics are three **Sun-Shards**)

1. **The Broken Choir** (2 floors): **Seraph-Commander Vael**.
   - Light-spear volleys and dive attacks.
   - Calls fallen seraphs.
2. **The Storm Spire** (2 floors, wind indoors): **the Tempest Drake**.
   - Lightning breath and storm rings.
   - The tower floor tilts and gusts blow.
3. **The Wheel of Eyes** (3 floors): **the Ophan Prime**, a huge wheel of eyes.
   - Rotating beams to dodge.
   - The eyes close one by one as you blind them.
4. **The Ashen Sanctum** (3 floors, needs the three Sun-Shards): **Solanthos, the Burnt-Out Sun**, a god who fell and has been burning down to ash ever since.
   - **Phase 1, Radiant:** solar flares, falling stars, burning ground.
   - **Phase 2, Ash:** the light goes out, an ash storm blinds the arena, and ember-wraiths rise.
   - **Phase 3, Eclipse:** he collapses into a dying core. Beams sweep the arena while the floor breaks away into the sky.
   - Afterwards: the ending and the credits, then Nightmare opens.

## Systems shared by both acts

- **Acts 5 and 6 get their own:** waypoints, quest log lines, a music track per town, relic HUD counts (PEARLS 0/3, SHARDS 0/3), and level scaling past Act 4.
- **Bot:** taught tides (prefer high ground) and edges (stay away from them).
  - `bot-acts.sh` grows to 8 heroes × 6 acts.
- **Items:** a few new set pieces and uniques themed to each act.
- **Debug flags:** `--act5` and `--act6`, like the existing `--act2` to `--act4`.
