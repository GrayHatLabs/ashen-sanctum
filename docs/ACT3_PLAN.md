# Act 3: The Mistwood (Ravenloft style)

Decided with the user on 2026-10-05:
- **Unlock:** after the Rime Wyrm. A road east of Kaldholm leads into the mists.
- **Size:** like Acts 1 and 2.
- **Boss:** **Count Vardak**.
- **Look:** a dark forest in neon green and blue-grey earth colours, a castle, and skeleton lords
  as the heralds (see `docs/ACT3_IDEAS.md`).

## Story

The Rime Wyrm's death broke the last of the old seals, and the mists to the east are rolling
west. In the village of **Mournhold**, the vampire hunter **Abelard** explains:
- **Count Vardak** rules the land from his castle on the cliff.
- His three skeleton lords hold the **Grave Sigils** that bar the castle gate.
- Kill them, take the sigils, storm the castle and end the Count.

## World

- **The Mistwood** (overland):
  - Blue-grey earth, glowing neon-green moss and mushrooms, twisted dead trees, gravestones.
  - Rolling fog and drifting will-o'-wisp motes.
  - Roaming ghouls, werewolves, wisps, cultists and banshees.
  - A road west to Kaldholm through the pass.
- **Mournhold**, a frightened village:
  - **Abelard** the hunter tells the story.
  - **Widow Kasia** sells potions, food and gear.
  - **Father Lucian** heals and resets skills.
  - Peasants.
- **Dungeons:**
  1. **The Sunken Chapel** (2 floors): **Lord Ossric, the Bone Baron**. He raises skeletons and
     throws volleys of bone spears.
  2. **The Gallows Catacombs** (2 floors): **Duchess Grimhilde**, a lich. Green necrotic fire
     volleys and pools of grave-fire, and she blinks away.
  3. **The Barrow of Knights** (3 floors): **Sir Malgrave**, a death knight. Shield charges,
     ground slams, and he calls up dead knights (skeletons).
  4. **Castle Vardak** (3 floors, needs the three sigils): **Count Vardak**.
     - **Phase 1:** blood-bolt fans and bat swarms.
     - **Phase 2:** he turns to mist (untouchable), reappears elsewhere, and calls wolves.
     - **Phase 3:** he takes the form of a giant bat. Faster, screeching shockwaves.

## Mechanics

- **Ghouls:** their claws numb you (the chill slow).
- **Werewolves:** they regenerate unless burning.
- **Banshees, wisps and cultists:** they cast green bolts.
- **Bloodless foes:** skeleton lords, banshees and wisps feed the vampire nothing.
- **Nightmare and Hell** move to after Count Vardak.
