# Act 3 ideas (from the user, 2026-10-05)

"An occult, Halloween-themed dark forest with skeleton lords."

Notes to build on when Act 3 starts:

- **Setting:**
  - A cursed, ever-autumn forest, maybe called "the Hollowwood".
  - Orange and violet light, jack-o'-lantern glows, crooked dead trees, fog.
  - Ritual circles and standing stones, hanging cages, a ruined chapel.
  - A witch-haunted village as the town.
- **Enemies:**
  - Skeleton lords: crowned skeletal nobles, the act's heralds and boss court.
  - Pumpkin-headed scarecrows, cultists in robes, will-o'-wisps, ghouls.
  - Black cats or crows as swarms.
- **Hook:** the occult rites in the forest are what woke the Ash King and the Rime Wyrm.
  - A coven of skeleton lords serves a final boss.
  - Final boss candidates: a lich-queen, the "Hollow King", or a great horned spirit of the wood.
- **The vampire class** fits this act naturally. The skeleton lords have no blood, which keeps it a challenge for her.
