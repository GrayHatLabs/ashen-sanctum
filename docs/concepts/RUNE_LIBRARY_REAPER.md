Stylized dark-fantasy illustration of a mysterious Gothic Rune Library Reaper, full body, standing silently between towering shelves inside an impossibly ancient forbidden library. She is not Death itself, but an immortal guardian charged with collecting lost knowledge, hunting those who steal forbidden texts, and guiding the spirits of scholars who die within the archive.

She has a calm, intimidating presence rather than a wild or aggressive one. Her expression is cold and intelligent, with a subtle knowing smile suggesting she has watched civilizations rise and disappear while guarding the same books.

She has extremely long silver-gray hair falling nearly to the floor in loose, slightly tangled waves. Several thin braids are woven through it with black thread, tiny antique keys, bone beads and tarnished silver rune charms.

Her skin is ghostly pale with faint gray undertones. Her eyes glow muted amber-gold against heavy charcoal smoky eyeshadow, sharp black eyeliner and dark black-plum lips.

Thin black rune markings descend from beneath one eye and continue along her neck, shoulders and arms. When spirits approach, the markings begin glowing faintly gold.

She wears a deep black hood attached to an enormous weathered reaper cloak. The cloak is constructed from overlapping layers of black wool, torn velvet, leather and translucent funeral fabric.

Its edges appear unnaturally dark, occasionally dissolving into drifting black smoke.

The interior lining is covered with thousands of tiny embroidered runes representing the names of scholars whose spirits she has collected.

Beneath the cloak she wears a fitted black leather gothic corset reinforced with aged bronze clasps and engraved rune plates. A high-slit layered black skirt falls around tall leather boots, combining Victorian mourning clothing with the practical equipment of an ancient archivist.

Multiple belts wrap around her waist carrying old keys, scroll tubes, hourglasses, ink bottles, sealing wax, rune stones, chains, miniature grimoires and mysterious locked leather cases.

Several ancient books hang from chains attached to her belt.

Each book contains the memories of someone who died protecting forbidden knowledge.

Her signature weapon is an enormous Rune Scythe.

The long shaft is made from ancient black wood wrapped in weathered leather and iron bands. Small chains, keys and hourglasses hang beneath its head.

Its enormous curved blade is forged from nearly black metal engraved with hundreds of glowing blue-white runes.

The runes are normally dormant.

When the Reaper hunts someone who has stolen forbidden knowledge, the symbols illuminate one by one until the entire blade burns with spectral blue light.

A small iron lantern hangs beneath the scythe blade.

Instead of fire, the lantern contains a swirling pale-blue spirit flame.

The souls of lost scholars occasionally appear inside the flame as faint faces before disappearing again.

She carries a massive chained grimoire called the Ledger of the Forgotten.

The book floats beside her rather than being carried normally. Its cracked black leather cover contains an ancient bronze circular lock surrounded by moving runes.

Whenever someone dies inside the library, the grimoire opens by itself.

A blank page fills with glowing handwriting recording their name, memories and final thoughts.

Behind the Reaper drift several translucent spirits of long-dead librarians, scholars and explorers. They silently follow her through the archive, occasionally pointing toward forgotten passages or dangerous books.

A large black raven accompanies her, usually sitting on the head of her scythe.

Its eyes glow the same muted amber as hers.

The forbidden library surrounding her is unimaginably vast.

Towering gothic bookshelves disappear upward into darkness while iron bridges, spiral staircases and narrow balconies connect different levels of the archive.

Thousands of candles illuminate the shelves, but entire sections remain completely dark.

Books occasionally float between shelves on their own.

Some are chained shut.

Others whisper.

A few breathe.

At the deepest point of the library stands an enormous black stone doorway covered with hundreds of locks, chains and overlapping magical seals.

Ancient blue runes glow around its frame.

The Rune Library Reaper guards this door above everything else.

Nobody remembers what lies behind it.

Not even the Rune Librarian.

The Reaper remembers.
