Stylized dark-fantasy illustration of a powerful Gothic Inquisitor woman, full body, standing within the shattered nave of an enormous ruined cathedral. She is an elite hunter of witches, demons, corrupted knights and cursed creatures, trained to identify supernatural corruption, mark it for judgment, and destroy it.

She is not a gentle priestess.

She represents judgment.

Her expression is cold, controlled and utterly confident, with the faintest hint of contempt toward the cursed creature standing before her. Even surrounded by supernatural horrors, she appears completely unafraid.

She has long ash-blonde hair with darker roots, falling in messy waves past her waist. Several narrow braids are woven with tiny iron chains, black beads and small religious charms.

Her skin is pale ivory and marked with several thin scars earned during previous hunts.

Her eyes are unusual: normally smoky gray, but when confronting cursed enemies they gradually illuminate with molten amber-gold light.

Heavy charcoal eyeshadow, sharp black eyeliner and dark brown-black lips give her a severe gothic appearance.

A black ceremonial hood frames her face.

Behind her head rises a distinctive Iron Halo of Judgment—a circular crown constructed from tarnished brass and blackened iron with long narrow spikes radiating outward like the rays of a corrupted sun.

Small chains and seals hang from the halo and move gently even when there is no wind.

Her armor combines gothic cathedral imagery with the equipment of a professional monster hunter.

She wears a fitted black leather battle corset reinforced with overlapping blackened-steel plates, brass buckles and engraved protective symbols.

A heavy black mantle covers her shoulders, constructed from layered leather, dark feathers and weathered fabric.

Beneath it hangs a long split inquisitorial coat made from black, charcoal and dirty ivory cloth with a deep oxblood lining.

Long strips of parchment covered in prayers, names and binding symbols are stitched directly into the coat.

Some are burned around the edges.

Others are stained from previous hunts.

Her arms are protected by mismatched blackened-steel gauntlets engraved with tiny prayers and warding symbols. Heavy chains wrap around one forearm, allowing her to control her primary weapon.

Tall black leather boots rise above her knees, reinforced with iron plates, buckles and small brass religious symbols.

Multiple belts surround her waist carrying relics, sealing wax, iron nails, holy oils, small books, chains, keys, antidotes and tools for identifying supernatural corruption.

Her signature weapon is the Censer of Judgment.

It is an enormous blackened-brass incense burner covered with spikes, engraved symbols and tiny openings that glow orange from the sacred coals burning inside.

The censer hangs from several feet of heavy chain.

In combat she swings it like a brutal flail.

Every impact releases clouds of burning incense, sparks and supernatural golden fire.

The smoke is harmless to ordinary people.

Cursed creatures find it agonizing.

Her most important ability is the Brand of Judgment.

By pointing toward a corrupted enemy, she causes a glowing sigil to burn itself into their flesh, armor or spectral form.

The brand resembles a circular gothic seal surrounded by sharp geometric symbols.

Once branded, the creature cannot easily hide its corruption.

The Inquisitor can sense it through walls, darkness and supernatural illusions.

More importantly, her power increases dramatically against branded cursed enemies.

The stronger the curse, the stronger she becomes.

Her eyes begin glowing.

The symbols engraved into her armor ignite with golden-orange light.

Smoke pours from the censer.

Her movements become faster and more powerful.

Against ordinary enemies she is dangerous.

Against heavily cursed creatures she becomes terrifying.

Her second technique is Censer Sweep.

She spins the chained censer around her body in enormous sweeping arcs, creating circles of burning incense and sparks. Enemies struck by the censer are staggered while cursed creatures ignite with supernatural flame.

Her Purification ability allows her to slam the censer into the ground, releasing a violent wave of burning incense through the surrounding area.

Minor curses are destroyed.

Powerful curses are weakened.

Possessed creatures may briefly regain control of themselves.

Her ultimate state is known as Final Judgment.

When facing an exceptionally powerful cursed creature, every brand she has placed begins glowing simultaneously.

Her Iron Halo ignites behind her head.

The parchment prayers attached to her clothing lift into the air.

The censer burns white-hot.

Golden symbols appear across the cathedral floor beneath her feet.

For several moments, she becomes the physical embodiment of judgment against supernatural corruption.

The stronger and more corrupted her opponent is, the more devastating she becomes.

Her environment is a ruined gothic cathedral converted into an Inquisitorial stronghold.

Hundreds of candles illuminate enormous stone arches while iron cages, chained books, monster-hunting equipment and sealed relics fill the side chambers.

Thick incense smoke hangs throughout the cathedral.

At the far end stands an enormous black altar surrounded by hundreds of melted candles.

Behind it is a massive stained-glass window depicting a faceless figure holding scales in one hand and a burning sword in the other.

Below the cathedral lies the Vault of the Condemned, where dangerous cursed artifacts are sealed behind iron doors covered with brands identical to those used by the Inquisitor.

Some nights, something inside knocks back.
